Rules 6–10 for Autonomous Workloads

These rules extend the core AI FinOps Framework (Rules 1–5) to systems where AI operates without human supervision — agentic coding, batch processing, automated PR review, CI/CD pipelines. Start with Rules 1–5 for model selection and cost governance, then apply Rules 6–10 for unsupervised operations.

Rule 6 · The Work / Time Tradeoff

Batch processing is 50% cheaper but has a 72-hour horizon. If your task doesn't need a response in under 4 hours, run in batch. Monitor queue depth to avoid SLA breaches. Batch discount: D(b) = 1 − b × 0.5.

Example: Running 5,000 test-generation jobs at batch fraction 70% saves $262/day vs on-demand. The tradeoff: results arrive in 8.2 hours instead of 4.3 minutes.

Rule 7 · The Workstream Capacity Constraint

Your maximum throughput is set by your budget, not your infrastructure. Max jobs/day = Budget / (Cost per job × (1 + retry_rate)). Double your budget, double your capacity — zero hiring required.

Rule 8 · The Escalation Cascade

In autonomous workloads, failures auto-escalate through model tiers: Flash → Sonnet → Opus → Human. A 1% escalation to human review can double your total cost. Design for under 1% escalation. Measured multiplier: 28.2× (DeepSeek → GPT-5.6).

Rule 9 · The 72-Hour Horizon

Actual batch completion time = (Queue_length × Avg_job_time) + (Retry_buffer × retry_rate). Never batch jobs with an SLA less than 2× your observed queue depth. Always have an on-demand fallback for time-critical tasks.

Rule 10 · The WOC Ratio

Work Completed / Work Offered = your efficiency metric. Below 0.85: investigate prompt design. Below 0.70: critical — fix immediately. Track WOC by task type, model, and time of day. Measured average across 248 experiments: 0.79 (21.4% overall failure rate).

WOC Ratio

The Work Overhead Cost ratio tells you whether autonomy is actually saving money.

WOC = (costvalidation + costretries + costescalations) / costprimary

Healthy

WOC < 0.70

Overhead is manageable. Scaling is safe.

Investigate

WOC < 0.85

Retry or escalation rate rising. Tune thresholds.

Critical

WOC ≥ 0.85

Overhead dominates. Pause autonomy until root cause found.

Measured average: WOC = 0.79 across 248 experiment sessions. This is in the "investigate" range — autonomy is viable but requires active monitoring. The 65× cost gap between Claude and DeepSeek is the dominant factor.

72-Hour Horizon · Batch SLA Management

Batch workloads don't need sub-second response times. A 72-hour window unlocks massive cost savings through scheduling optimization.

Queue Depth

Number of jobs waiting. If queue_depth > 2 × daily throughput, you need more capacity or a longer window.

Slack Buffer

Reserve 20% of the window for retries. At 21.4% retry rate, a 72h window needs ~14.4h of slack.

Completion Time Formula

Tcomplete = Njobs / Rthroughput + Tslack

Guarantees delivery within the SLA window.
