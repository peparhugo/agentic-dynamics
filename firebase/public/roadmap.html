Research Note: EPM Shock Scenario Modeling — The current EPM model assumes a linear 1.6%/yr IEA baseline, but real energy markets experience non-linear shocks: ERCOT freeze events, EU carbon pricing spikes, Asian LNG supply disruptions. A step-function EPM jump compounds through the Snowball Rule (N²) — a 2× EPM spike during a 5-year horizon can double cumulative costs overnight. Modeling stochastic EPM scenarios and their compound interaction with codebase growth is a v0.3+ research priority. Interested in energy economics or financial modeling? Get involved →
Research Note: v/ε Entanglement — Velocity (v) cannot be measured independently of the Explanation Tax (ε). A model that narrates heavily generates more total tokens per session, which inflates apparent velocity if measured by token output, or deflates it if measured by useful AST nodes. The narration trail also bloats context history, accelerating the N² tax through a separate pathway — meaning β is itself a function of ε and v. Decoupling these variables requires a factorial experiment: hold ε constant across models, vary v, and measure the resulting β. This is a measurement methodology problem, not a page edit — it belongs in a paper or a dedicated calibration study. v0.3+ research priority.

How to Contribute

Items marked Help Wanted are actively seeking external runs. Use the filter above to see only those.

  1. Fork: github.com/peparhugo/ai-finops-framework
  2. Configure: Add a config in experiments/configs/
  3. Run: python scripts/run.py --config your-config.yaml
  4. Submit: Open a PR with your results. We verify and merge.

Track active tasks on GitHub Issues. If you have API credits or want to coordinate on a specific pillar, reach out there. We’re exploring grant funding to sponsor community experiments.
