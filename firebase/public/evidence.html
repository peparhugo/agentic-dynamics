3 · Verification tracks vendor, not price

OpenAI spans a ~43× price range (Luna $0.09 → Sol $3.99) with tests flat at 10–12 per story. Claude spans ~3× (Haiku $1.54 → Sonnet 5 $4.78) with tests flat at ~150–190. You do not buy more tests by paying for a pricier sibling; you buy them by switching vendors.

The verification gap

An order of magnitude, between vendors

Luna authors ~10 tests/story that confirm the happy path; Sonnet 5 authors ~153 that probe edge cases. Both codebases "work." One has a much broader observed verification surface.

Does verification pay?

Weakly protective, at best

Correlation of tests-per-story with reviewer-flagged "worse" commits is r = −0.154 — more tests predict slightly fewer bad commits, but weakly. Tests and reviewer-judged quality are largely independent signals.

Cleanliness is decoupled from cost

Paying more buys neither fewer diagnostics nor higher cleanliness

The cheapest model (Flash, $0.068) has the lowest code-quality score (0.037); Claude Haiku ($1.54) has the best (0.037→ the ordering is not monotonic in price). Cost and cleanliness are independent axes.

4 · A small correctness edge at ~73× the cost

Across 15 overlapping task types in the perturbation corpus, the premium model leads on 7 and the cheaper model on 3 (5 tie) — a ~5-point average correctness edge bought at ~73× the cost.

5 · What the agent leaves behind

An independent reviewer (DeepSeek flash) scored every story; static analysis parsed the produced code. The agent's default failure modes are condition-independent — they are the agent's signature, not degradation artifacts.

6 · The instrumented fields

Four signals that were previously absent from the ledger — and that therefore gated the policy arms that consume them — are now measured. This closes the loop the architecture demanded.

FieldWhat it measuresUnlocks
confidencePer-attempt confidence signalmodel_cascade / dynamics
perturbation_strengthControlled perturbation magnitude sgrit (strength axis)
test_executed_successIndependent pass/fail from the runnergrit (verified success)
answer / explanationOutput-token split (deliverable vs narration)output decomposition
The answer/explanation split is the output decomposition. Reasoning is the cost, not narration — and it is a reasoning-token channel, not a narration penalty. Measuring it as a ledger field turns "how much did the model narrate" into a computable decomposition of the output. [M]

7 · The instrument self-heals

On 2026-08-15, an async worker — given a single high-level goal and no per-step steering — diagnosed the repository's own data contamination, instrumented the four missing fields, recomputed the derived metrics, and then authored and executed a new experiment to re-run the contaminated cells.
