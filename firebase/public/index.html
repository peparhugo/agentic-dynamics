How we know what we know

Transparent methodology

10 perturbation operators, 6 recovery signals, fully documented protocols. Every measurement traceable to original session data.

Multi-model

8 variants across 3 architectures — GPT dense, DeepSeek MoE, Claude dense. Cross-architecture comparison at the AST level.

Replicable

Fully open source under MIT license. 34 configs, 251 worktrees. The instrument runs locally — no API keys required to verify.

What we're honest about

Sample size limits

248 sessions across 8 models means some variants have limited coverage. Framework extensions (Rules 6–11) await broader validation.

Single-domain bias

Validated on Python/Flask CRUD workloads. TypeScript baselines exist (21 sessions) but full cross-language validation is underway.

Static snapshot risk

Model APIs, pricing, and behavior change. A cost framework measured today may not hold in 6 months without recalibration.
