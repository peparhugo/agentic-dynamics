Your AI bill grows. You can’t forecast it.
You can’t govern it. This framework changes that.

Your costs are invisible

Most teams measure cost per prompt — a metric that ignores pass rates, codebase growth compounding, and energy price inflation.

Your model routing is guesswork

Without calibrated pass-rate data, you can’t know whether switching models saves or costs you money over a 3-year horizon.

Your budget runway is unmodeled

Codebase growth compounds quadratically (N²). The β·v·N² term dominates long-run cost — model the curve before architectural commitments.

How the framework works

1. Assess

Measure your current cost-to-serve per task type. Establish baseline WOC ratio, escalation rate, and model utilization profile.

2. Tune

Apply the 11 rules. Implement cascade routing, cache layering, and budget enforcement. Target: 50–70% cost reduction.

3. Dashboard

Track cost-per-outcome, throughput, and budget runway in real time. Prove ROI to stakeholders with the four-dimension executive dashboard.
