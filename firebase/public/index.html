Original contribution

What the survey cannot tell you: how models behave when the input degrades.

Every LLM can look competent on a clean benchmark. Production is not clean. Requirements are incomplete, stale, contradictory, or framed in unfamiliar language. This framework measures whether a model preserves ground truth under that stress, and what recovery costs.

01

Grit

Ground-Truth Integrity: correctness maintained under degraded, contradictory, or incomplete instructions.

02

Explanation Tax

The cost of narration, flailing, and recovery when a model externalizes reasoning instead of acting.

03

WOC

Work-outcome conversion: first-pass success as the budgeting metric, not cost per prompt.

04

Snowball Rule

Codebase growth compounds future context cost. The N² term turns small routing choices into runway risk.
