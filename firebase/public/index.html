1. Grit [M]
Default to models that maintain correctness under degraded input. Low flail rate = high grit.
2. Explanation Tax [M]
Measure what resilience costs. Claude: 11% flail + 8.5% narration penalty. DeepSeek: 8% flail, zero penalty.
3. Snowball Rule [C]
Codebase growth compounds quadratically (N²). Model the curve before architectural commitments.
4. EPM Horizon [X]
Energy costs are the inflation rate of AI compute. Find the year prices flip your model selection.
5. First-Pass Rule [M]
Price the outcome, not the prompt. WOC = 1/(1+r). Measured WOC = 0.90.
6. Batch Discount [M]
Batch processing is 50% cheaper. 72-hour horizon. Modeled from WFM principles.
7. Budget Ceiling [C]
Max jobs/day = Budget / (Cost × (1+r)). Throughput is budget-constrained, not infra-constrained.
8. Cascade Rule [M]
Failures auto-escalate through model tiers. Design for <1% human escalation. Modeled from WFM tiered support.
9. SLA Buffer [M]
Batch completion = queue depth × avg time + retry buffer. Never batch within 2× queue depth of SLA.
10. Outcome Multiplier [C]
Maximize outcomes per dollar. BVI = Total Successful Outcomes / Total AI + Human Cost.

Original contribution

What the survey cannot tell you: how models behave when the input degrades.

Every LLM can look competent on a clean benchmark. Production is not clean. Requirements are incomplete, stale, contradictory, or framed in unfamiliar language. This framework measures whether a model preserves ground truth under that stress, and what recovery costs.

01

Grit

Ground-Truth Integrity: correctness maintained under degraded, contradictory, or incomplete instructions.

02

Explanation Tax

The cost of narration, flailing, and recovery when a model externalizes reasoning instead of acting.

03

WOC

Work-outcome conversion: first-pass success as the budgeting metric, not cost per prompt.

04

Snowball Rule

Codebase growth compounds future context cost. The N² term turns small routing choices into runway risk.
