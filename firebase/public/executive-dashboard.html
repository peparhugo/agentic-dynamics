What to Measure

Per Task

  • Cost per completed outcome
  • First-pass success rate
  • Model tier used
  • Retry count (if any)
  • Time to completion

Per Workstream

  • Daily / weekly / monthly spend
  • Throughput (jobs / day)
  • WOC ratio trend
  • Escalation rate trend
  • Budget vs actual variance

Per Budget Period

  • Total spend vs budget
  • Cost-by-model breakdown
  • Cost-by-workstream breakdown
  • Batch savings (cost avoided)
  • Projected runway remaining

Consulting Delivery · Build Your Scalability Engine

1

Baseline

2-week instrument audit. We deploy the measurement apparatus across your current AI pipelines. Output: baseline cost-per-task, WOC ratio, escalation profile, and model utilization report.

2

Tune

4-week optimization sprint. Implement cascade routing, cache layering, budget enforcement, and batch scheduling. Target: 50–70% cost reduction while maintaining or improving quality.

2-week executive instrumentation. Custom dashboards with the four dimensions above, alerting on WOC drift, budget overruns, and escalation anomalies. Handoff with runbook and training.
