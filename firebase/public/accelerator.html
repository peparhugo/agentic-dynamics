What is the Accelerator?

The Accelerator is an implementation guide and measurement harness — open-source Python tooling that instruments your AI sessions to measure cost-per-outcome across models. It is not a SaaS product. It runs locally against your sessions.

The Problem

You've deployed AI. You don't have acceleration. Here's why.

When AI works without human supervision — the rules, economics, and operational model

How Grit Controls Autonomous Workloads

The Grit framework directly governs unsupervised AI operations. In autonomous mode, you can't review every output. You need models that self-correct when given bad context — because bad context is inevitable in real systems (stale documentation, conflicting requirements, incomplete specifications).

  • Flail Rate determines which models should NEVER be in autonomous cascades. GPT-5-nano (14% flail) and GPT-5-mini (8% flail) cannot be trusted unsupervised.
  • Recovery Cost compounds across thousands of autonomous sessions. Claude writes 22,500 tokens to cache per session at $3.75/Mtok. At 5,000 jobs/day, that's $1,688/day in cache overhead alone. Switch to cache-read for autonomous batch processing.
  • First-Pass Success (Rule 5) IS Grit in production. Track WOC by model, task type, and time of day.

WOC Ratio — Rule 5 (First-Pass)

WOC = 1/(1+r) where r is retry rate. Higher WOC = better (more first-pass success). Measured: WOC = 0.90 from r=0.115.

Healthy

WOC > 0.85

Good first-pass rate. Scaling is safe.

Investigate

WOC 0.70–0.85

Retry rate rising. Tune thresholds.

Critical

WOC < 0.70

Too many retries. Pause autonomy until root cause found.

10 perturbation operators

4 manifold (alien vocab, framing shifts, causality reversal, forced abandonment) and 6 semantic (false premises, inverted constraints, removed constraints, phantom success, competing goals, contradiction injection).

6 recovery signals

Surface markers, behavioral convergence, qualifiers, tech term convergence, step length convergence, structural pattern convergence. Every trajectory step classified: EXPLORATION, RECOVERY, STABLE, or UNKNOWN.
