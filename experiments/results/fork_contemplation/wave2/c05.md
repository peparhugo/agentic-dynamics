**Entry claim (falsifiable):** Of the six pre-merge defects observed in this arc, at least four are detectable by a purely mechanical differential/interrogation rail at ≤$0.05 per finding with ≥95% specificity on a clean config corpus — so "the human review is the highest-yield oracle" conflates *absolute recall* (5/5 unique behavioral findings) with *marginal yield per unit cost*, and the largest unhedged risk is not that the human is unpinned but that **no reviewer — human or machine — has a calibration record (a probability-of-detection × specificity pair measured on a known defect population)**.

## Lens routing (two, kept in conflict before any synthesis)

**Lens L1 — Metrology / measurement traceability.** An oracle is an instrument. The runtime (`opencode` v1.18.15) is a *reference standard*: its output is observable and reproducible. A detector is *traceable* only if you can show its readings derive from that standard under stated conditions (gauge R&R, repeatability, reproducibility, uncertainty). On L1's reading the human pre-merge review is an **uncalibrated comparator**: it produced 5/5 true findings but has no measured POD, no measured specificity (no clean corpus was ever run through it), and no uncertainty statement. L1's remedy: replace the comparator with a traceable differential, calibrate it against the standard, publish uncertainty.

**Lens L2 — Inspection & reliability economics.** Inspection is a portfolio allocation: spend inspection where `escape_cost × POD_gap` is largest, and never assume one inspector catches everything (common-mode failure). On L2's reading the human review is a **high-sensitivity, low-throughput, single-point-of-common-mode** inspection station whose demonstrated POD on the behavioral subclass is 5/5 while the automated station's POD on the same subclass is 0/5 (CI caught only the static defect). L2's remedy: **do not retire a detector with demonstrated POD until the replacement's POD is measured on the same defect population**, and keep an independent inspector for the residual — the regress terminates not in a higher reviewer but in *ground truth* (the reference standard) plus an accountable acceptance owner.

**Where they conflict (kept visible):** L1 says retire the human as soon as the differential is traceable; L2 says a live, spending run (`run-0da5271bc0cb`) makes an escape far more expensive than one review, so retirement is *conditional on a no-regression benchmark*. I resolve this later by staged demotion, not replacement — and I do not blend them before stating the measurement.

## 1. Quantification of marginal yield (with the honesty about n)

Define the defect population **D = {D1…D6}** for this arc, classified by the *kind of authority* a detector would need:

| id | defect (compressed) | authority needed | mechanizable? | mechanism |
|----|--------------------|------------------|---------------|-----------|
| D1 | completed compaction summary usage read as the new context | **runtime state semantics** (its run loop skips `summary === true`) | yes (behavioral probe) | drive a forked session through compaction; assert our `_measure` matches the observed native continuation |
| D2 | config order / partial-limit merge / JSONC trailing commas | **runtime config resolution** | yes (direct diff) | generated config permutations vs `opencode models <provider> --verbose` |
| D3 | `reasoning` added to the missing-`total` sum | **runtime formula text** | yes (golden test) | golden assertion of `input+output+cache.read+cache.write` |
| D4 | override claims native compaction / unbounded override | **our own policy invariants** (no runtime analog) | yes (invariant test) | `COMPACT ⟹ context ≥ native_effective_limit`; `policy_limit ≤ native_effective_limit` |
| D5 | corrupt DB crashes the CLI | **robustness class** | yes (fault test) | malformed-db input, assert structured UNJUDGED |
| D6 (*) | F401 missing `__all__` | static | already mechanical | ruff (`bc87e60af`) |

(*) D6 is listed to make the recall denominators honest; it was caught by machinery, not by the human.

**Findings per review.** One human review instance, five findings → point estimate **5.0 findings/review** (95% interval is enormous at n=1; treat as a single Bernoulli outcome, not a rate). Of those, **5/5 were behavioral and 0 were static**. What machinery caught on its own: D6 only → **automated recall on D = 1/6 ≈ 17%; human unique-finding count = 5**.

**Cost per finding.**
- *Automated (measured):* the ten PR checks cost minutes of runner time (cents); ruff's share of D6 is ≈ $0 marginal.
- *Human (estimated, not measured):* if the review consumed 30–60 minutes of controller attention at a loaded €60–150/h, total is ~€30–150 → **~€6–30 per finding**. I am explicitly labeling this an *assumption*, because the arc never recorded review wall-clock, tokens, or finding count in a schema — that missing measurement is itself a finding (see §4).
- *Projected differential:* D2/D3/D4/D5 are seconds of local compute and a handful of assertions; D1 needs one forked-session probe. Using the arc's own measured fork cost (`c50f37cfff42523b`: 4 isolated branches for $0.004735) as the upper bound for a behavioral probe, the mechanizable class lands at **<$0.05/finding**, i.e. **2–3 orders of magnitude below** the human estimate. That gap is the marginal-yield argument in one number.

**What it caught that machinery did not.** All five are *divergence from the reference standard* (D1–D3) or *internal policy invariants* (D4–D5). The human's irreplaceable act was not catching them — it was **naming the dimension** ("compare to what OpenCode does; the helper sharing a file is not sharing an authority"). Once named, each check is mechanical. That is the whole residual definition below.

**The unpinned part, restated.** There is no artifact anywhere recording a reviewer's POD or specificity. The reviewer is simultaneously the most *sensitive* detector and the only *unmeasured* one, and the same statement is true of the differential rail we are about to build. "Unpinned" is therefore not a property of the human; it is a property of the *review function as a whole*.

Wave-1 **c17** proposed the "standing differential interrogator" and **Q-B** proposed scoring it against the reviewer's five findings. My delta is threefold and specific:

### 3.2 Reviewer calibration — `scripts/reviewer_calibration.py` (answers "who reviews the reviewer")

Design decision: make the reviewer's reviewer a **ground-truth benchmark**, not another reviewer. The regress terminates at the reference standard (runtime behavior) and at seeded labels — both of which are reproducible and cannot be argued with.

```python
#!/usr/bin/env python3
"""Score ANY reviewer against a seeded defect corpus -- human or model, same scale.

WHY
    "The human review is the highest-yield oracle" is currently unfalsifiable: nothing
    records a reviewer's probability-of-detection (POD), specificity, or cost. This
    harness supplies the missing calibration record, and it is what terminates the
    "who reviews the reviewer" regress: the reviewer's reviewer is GROUND TRUTH
    (seeded defects with labels + runtime behavior), not a higher reviewer.

INPUTS
    --corpus   experiments/definitions/reviewer_faults/<version>/   # versioned, committed
    --reviewer <id>                                                  # identity on the record
    --findings <review-output.json>                                  # the reviewer's findings
    --out      experiments/results/reviews/calibration/

SCORING (all computed, never narrated)
    recall      = |findings ∩ seeds| / |seeds|          # POD
    precision   = |findings ∩ seeds| / |findings|
    specificity = 1 - false_alarms / |clean_patches|    # needs a clean control corpus
    cost        = wall_clock_s and, for model reviewers, the ledger $ for the review turn
    common_mode = reviewer provider/family vs author provider/family (must differ)

ANTI-OVERFIT (design decisions)
    * The live seed set is split: a published train set (used to tune any detector) and a
      HELD-OUT set never shown to a detector under development. A detector that only pass
      the published set is not calibrated; the held-out recall is the number that counts.
    * Clean patches are injected so a reviewer that always screams is caught by specificity.
    * Review input is the diff + authority outputs ONLY -- never the author's rationale --
      to prevent the reviewer from grading its agreement with the author instead of truth.
    * A rotation schedule bumps the corpus version; recall is charted per version so
      overfitting shows up as a sawtooth (train up, held-out flat).
"""
```

### 3.3 Adoption policy (the L1×L2 resolution)

| stage | gate behavior | human review role | promotion criterion |
|-------|---------------|-------------------|---------------------|
| 0 — shadow | differential runs, reports, never blocks | unchanged (all PRs) | harness green on v1 corpus |

Design decision: the promotion threshold is *relative* (detector POD ≥ human POD) rather than an absolute, because the arc supplies exactly one human POD observation and an absolute bar would be arbitrary. Design decision: stage 1 blocks only the mechanizable class; the human is *demoted*, never *retired on faith* (L2), and never during a live spending run unless the benchmark says so.

## 4. Residual risk: explicit acceptance, owner, and independence

Let me define the residual precisely, because the arc's own evidence says it is ≈1 finding/review, not 5:

> **Residual R** = defects that are neither authority-differential nor invariant-checkable: (a) *dimension selection* — knowing that "compare to the runtime" is the thing to check at all; (b) *genuine requirement ambiguity* and risk acceptance (how much to spend, what "done" means); (c) *adversarial gaming* not present in the seed corpus.

**Owner:** the controller. **Rule:** the permanence/approval artifact must carry an explicit residual line — either `residual: none named` or the count and classes plus the acceptance rationale. An implicit residual is exactly what the arc had (the human's value was real but invisible to the process, so it could not be budgeted, delegated, or audited). **Who reviews the reviewer:**
- for the mechanizable class — the **calibration harness** (§3.2), ground truth = seeds + runtime;
- for the harness itself — the controller, on a schedule, against a **held-out** seed set the detector never saw (this is the termination of the regress: the controller audits the *harness*, not every finding);
- for the residual class — an **independent** reviewer (different provider/family from the author; no author rationale in the input) plus the controller's signature; independence is a *measured field* in the calibration record, not an aspiration;
- **common-mode control** — never let the author's family review its own class of change silently; the record's `common_mode.independent` field makes a same-family review visible.

**What this explicitly does not solve (kept visible):** new *dimensions* of correctness cannot be seeded before they are known; the residual owner is therefore a permanent, named cost — the correct output of this analysis is to make it *bounded and priced*, not to claim it is eliminated.
