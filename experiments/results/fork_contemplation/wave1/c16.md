## Synthesis: the load-bearing move and its scale profile

**Leading claim.** The value in this arc was not "raise the budget"; it was **replacing a local re-implementation of an external authority with consumption of that authority, and making every consumer share one resolved judgment** (CLI, capsule, exec-boundary gate; `session_capacity` as the single resolver). The corollary safety property: after the five fixes, the gate is never more restrictive than the installed runtime *for capacity reasons*.

**Scale-dependent choices (what breaks at 10×).**
- *Concurrency:* the claim lane's single-writer assumption (`_recover_processing`), the broker as sole, blocking Docker caller (`docker compose run` waits for the whole run), the dispatch pool default of 4 (`FINOPS_FLEET_DISPATCH_WORKERS`), lease-registry contention, and per-check cost (a ~1.9s `opencode models` subprocess plus a scan of a 93GB `message` table per judgment) all become throughput walls. At 1× they are invisible; at 10× they set the ceiling.
- *Budgets:* the per-check subprocess and the DB scan consume budget on the measurement path itself; a shared env override becomes a fleet-wide, model-blind knob; campaign/portfolio allocation (seven promotables) is the scarce resource.
- *Isolation:* per-run repo clones (`/tmp/agentic-dynamics-runs/run-0da5271bc0cb/repo`) and per-cell `/state` scale linearly in disk/IO; the session DB is a *non-forkable singleton* (the KB frozen-snapshot finding is the proof), so measurement has no per-operator isolation; `session_budget.jsonl` and `packet_reads.jsonl` are global append-only files.
- *Review capacity:* the human semantic oracle is the bottleneck; the durable mitigation is to mechanize wherever an oracle exists (the runtime CLI for config semantics, the control DB for run state) and reserve humans for judgment.
- *Knowledge relevance:* transport invariants such as goals-ride-argv, newlines refused, untracked specs never reach clones (`6ef9bf9b6ef3b53a`) are hard and scale-invariant; the prompt-branch negative result (`7948b8ace287e881`) warns against policy-as-prose; cache ratios (95.5–97.9%, 47→90%) are the economics of fan-out.
