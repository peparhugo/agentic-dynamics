Named research question

What changes when an agent inherits the world it helped create?

Agentic Dynamics asks how task conditions, environment, workflow, and time change an agent's accepted outcomes, verification, recovery, cost, and downstream consequences.

Five boundaries keep the question honest.

Outcome: passing a self-authored test is not independent verification. Time: a later session inherits prior code and context. Cost: price, tokens, cache behavior, and recovery are not one variable. Policy: a proposed route is not an observed improvement. Scope: this instrument currently studies coding-agent workflows, not every agentic domain.

These boundaries make a field question rather than a vendor comparison. They also determine what cannot yet be claimed.

Policy is downstream of information.

A control rule may consume only fields measurement produces. The compiler enforces that ordering.

Openable claims

Every answer needs a method and a limit.
