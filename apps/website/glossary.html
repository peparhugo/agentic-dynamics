Definitions / source boundaries

Terms are not conclusions.

Each term names a measure, a method, a model, or a proposal. Definitions do not inherit stronger evidence than their source.

Grit

Outcome retention under perturbation: G(s) = P(test_executed_success | perturbation_strength=s). It requires an independent test verdict and a recorded strength; missing fields are excluded.

[M] inputs; [C] proportion. Evidence page formal definition.

Cell

One controlled trial: workflow plus a factor assignment and instrumentation. A fixed workflow run is one cell; an experiment varies factors across cells.

[P] experiment-spec vocabulary.

Policy arm

A control choice included as a factor level in a grid. A proposal is not a result; an arm can be measured or decided only through its registered comparison.

[P] policy design; see Framework.

Cost per verified outcome

A computed measure using captured cost and independently verified accepted outcomes. It is not cost per prompt and does not erase quality or latency.

[M] inputs / [C] result; `cap_2b` uses CPVO.

E_x

The ratio of escalation-fix cost to original-cell cost. The measured campaign reports 11.4671 for Sol and 12.5134 for Sonnet, each at n=1 model cell.

[M] costs / [C] ratio; escalation score JSON.

Null / untriggered

A named absence: not measured, unavailable, or a mechanism whose trigger did not fire. It is neither a zero nor proof that an effect is absent.

[M] coverage or trigger state.

How to read a claim.

[M] MEASURED direct capture. [C] COMPUTED transformation of captured inputs. [H] HEURISTIC thresholded or algorithmic classification. [X] EXTERNAL outside source. [P] POLICY decision, design, or proposal.
